FAQ / POLICY

Know the boundaries.

Can this download any webpage video?+

No. It accepts eligible direct media-file URLs. It intentionally does not bypass platform restrictions, login gates, DRM, or protected streaming systems.

Why does a normal Dailymotion or YouTube page fail?+

Those are platform pages rather than direct media-file URLs. This build blocks major platform hosts instead of extracting protected streams.

Can I deploy this publicly?+

Yes, but production deployment should add rate limiting, stronger DNS-rebinding protection, monitoring, abuse controls, HTTPS, and host-level bandwidth limits.
