Questions

FAQ

Does NeonFetch really support 100+ websites?

The extraction engine contains a large catalog of site-specific extractors plus direct-file handling. Site support changes over time, so an individual URL can still fail when a website changes its player or requires authentication.

Can it download Dailymotion videos?

It can inspect publicly accessible Dailymotion URLs when the extractor can access a downloadable non-DRM format. Private, paid, login-required or protected media is rejected.

Why do I see fewer quality choices than the website player?

NeonFetch only shows formats that can be delivered without requesting your credentials or bypassing access controls. Some services expose higher resolutions as separate protected or account-dependent streams.

Does NeonFetch store the video?

The service is intended to stream or process the selected media on demand rather than maintain a permanent download library.
