Questions

FAQ

Can NeonFetch download authorized YouTube videos?

Yes, when the video is accessible without bypassing DRM, private access, login requirements, subscriptions, or other technical access controls. The available quality options are shown after analysis.

Can it produce 1080p or 4K?

When the source exposes non-DRM high-resolution video plus an accessible audio stream, NeonFetch can download both and merge them into a single file with FFmpeg.

Why can some YouTube URLs still fail?

Some videos require authentication, age verification, paid access, region-specific access, or use protections that the service intentionally does not circumvent.

Does the server keep my downloaded video?

No permanent library is created. High-quality merged downloads use temporary ephemeral files during processing and delete them after delivery.
